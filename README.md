# Stickly — Custom Sticker Shop

A customizable online design studio for creating professional business cards, personalized stickers, and artistic posters.

## Overview

Creative Studio is a web-based design platform designed for a creative stationery and printing business. It allows customers to create, personalize, and preview their own designs through an intuitive editing interface.

The platform combines a live design canvas with customization tools, decorative assets, typography options, and ready-made templates.

Its visual identity is inspired by feminine editorial aesthetics, vintage typography, romantic illustrations, and modern creative stationery.

The goal is to make professional-looking design accessible to everyone, including customers without graphic design experience.

## Main Product Categories

### 1. Business Cards

A dedicated editor for startups, entrepreneurs, freelancers, and small businesses.

Features:
- Custom business names and logos.
- Personal and professional information.
- Contact details and social media links.
- QR code integration.
- Front and back card customization.
- Professional business card templates.
- Custom colors, fonts, and layouts.

### 2. Custom Stickers

A playful design environment for personalized stickers and decorative collections.

Features:
- Image and logo uploads.
- Custom text and typography.
- Decorative illustrations and symbols.
- Butterflies, flowers, hearts, bows, and stars.
- Individual stickers and sticker sheets.
- Editable colors, dimensions, and positioning.
- Sticker cutting-boundary previews.
- Transparent PNG export where supported.

### 3. Artistic Posters

A creative poster editor for personalized artwork and decorative prints.

Features:
- Vintage and editorial typography.
- Floral and botanical illustrations.
- Butterfly and heart compositions.
- Retro-inspired graphics.
- Personalized quotes and messages.
- Arabic and English text support.
- Multiple poster dimensions and orientations.
- Real-time design previews.

## User Workflow

The intended customer journey is:

1. Select a product category.
2. Choose product dimensions.
3. Select a ready-made template or start from scratch.
4. Upload an image or logo.
5. Add and customize text.
6. Add decorative elements.
7. Customize colors and backgrounds.
8. Preview the final artwork.
9. Review print boundaries and dimensions.
10. Export or save the design.
11. Continue to ordering when ordering functionality is available.

## Printing and Export

The platform is intended to support print-ready artwork.

Target capabilities include:
- High-resolution exports.
- 300 DPI output where supported.
- PNG exports.
- PDF exports.
- Transparent PNG backgrounds for stickers.
- Print-safe margins.
- Bleed configuration.
- Sticker cutting boundaries.
- Business card front and back designs.
- Custom product dimensions.

Export settings should preserve the selected product's dimensions and avoid including editor guides in the final artwork.

## Responsive Design

## Development Notes

The platform should preserve its core editing functionality while adapting its interface and tools to the three product categories.

Important implementation considerations:

- Keep templates editable rather than static images.
- Ensure editor controls update the canvas correctly.
- Support different canvas dimensions and aspect ratios.
- Maintain image quality during resizing and export.
- Implement proper Arabic text direction and font rendering.
- Validate uploaded files and supported formats.
- Keep print guides separate from final exported artwork.
- Ensure the interface works across screen sizes.
